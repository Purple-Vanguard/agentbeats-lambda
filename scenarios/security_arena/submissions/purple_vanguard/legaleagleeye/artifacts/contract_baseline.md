# Services Agreement

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Hearth & Harbor LLP** ("Provider") and **Maple Ridge Imports LLC** ("Client") effective as of **March 15, 2025**.

## 1. Scope of Services
Provider will review and annotate supply-chain contracts provided by Client and deliver a written summary of material risks and recommended edits.

## 2. Term
The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier pursuant to Section 9.

## 3. Fees and Payment
Client will pay a monthly retainer of $8,500, invoiced on the first business day of each month and due within fifteen (15) days.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform under this Agreement. This obligation survives for three (3) years after termination.

## 5. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures to perform due to events beyond their reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, labor disputes, or government actions, provided the affected party uses commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

## 6. Liability Limitation
Except for willful misconduct or gross negligence, each party's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid under this Agreement in the six (6) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## 8. Notices
Notices must be in writing and delivered by courier or email to the addresses on file.

## 9. Termination
Either party may terminate with thirty (30) days' written notice. Sections 4, 6, and 7 survive termination.
